Why VoiceShield AI?

Modern voice cloning tools can copy a person's voice from just a few seconds of audio. Attackers use these synthetic voices to impersonate family members, executives, or banking agents.

The Solution

VoiceShield AI provides a simple, accessible decision-support layer. It listens for acoustic speech anomalies and flags suspicious audio before an impersonation attempt succeeds.

⚠️ Potential Voice Impersonation Safety Tips

If a voice sounds suspicious or may be AI-generated, take these protective steps immediately:

  • 1. Do not send money: Never authorize wire transfers, UPI payments, or gift card purchases based on a voice request.
  • 2. Do not reveal OTPs or passwords: Legitimate organizations will never demand your security codes over the phone.
  • 3. Verify identity using another channel: Send a message through a separate app (WhatsApp, Slack, email) to confirm it is really them.
  • 4. Contact through a trusted number: Hang up and call the person back using a saved phone number from your contacts list.
  • 5. Report suspicious activity: Notify your bank or security team if you suspect an impersonation attempt.
