Architecture & Process

How It Works

A multi-layered acoustic pipeline designed for real-time voice cloning detection.

01

Capture

User records live voice via Web Audio API or uploads an audio file (WAV, MP3, WebM, OGG). The sample is pre-processed ephemerally in memory.

02

Analyze

Signal processing algorithms extract fundamental frequency (F0) contours, spectral roll-off, zero-crossing rates, and respiration intervals.

03

Detect

Neural classifier modules evaluate spectral gating, robotic pitch constancy, vocoder phase dispersion, and synthetic diffusion footprints.

04

Protect

Delivers an instant 0–100 risk score, actionable defensive steps, and logs a tamper-evident SHA-256 digest to the verification ledger.

Problem Statement

With the rapid proliferation of few-shot neural text-to-speech models and generative voice cloning systems (e.g. ElevenLabs, VALL-E, Bark), cybercriminals can replicate an individual’s voice using only a few seconds of public audio. These clones power high-yield social engineering attacks, CEO wire transfer fraud, grandparent ransom scams, and customer support impersonations.

Our Solution

VoiceShield AI provides an accessible, non-intrusive decision-support verification layer. By combining acoustic signal extraction (measuring pitch jitter, spectral roll-off, respiration rhythm, and vocoder phase noise) with cryptographic SHA-256 tamper-evident logging, it delivers fast risk assessments and clear response protocols.

Data Protection & Privacy

Voice Data Privacy Policy

Voice data is sensitive biometric information. VoiceShield AI processes all audio responsibly.

1. Clear Consent Before Access

Your microphone is only accessed when you explicitly click "Start Recording" or "Start Live Verification" and approve browser permissions.

2. Ephemeral In-Memory Processing

Voice recordings are processed in ephemeral memory for the duration of the acoustic feature extraction and are not stored permanently by default.

3. User-Controlled History & Deletion

Verification history logs are stored locally on your device. You have full control to view individual records or clear your history at any time.

4. Cryptographic Proofs Only on Ledger

No raw voice audio is ever stored on the blockchain. Only irreversible SHA-256 mathematical hashes are recorded to verify tamper-evident integrity.

Out-of-Band Verification Guide

When a voice sample is classified as Suspicious or Possible AI-Generated, follow these recommended cybersecurity verification protocols:

  • 1. Hang up and call back: Never use the phone number the caller provides. Call back using a verified internal contact directory or previously known telephone number.
  • 2. Request a secondary channel confirmation: Ask the person to confirm their request via authenticated enterprise chat (Slack/Teams) or signed corporate email.
  • 3. Use a pre-agreed challenge phrase: For high-value transactions or family emergencies, ask a shared personal question that an AI model cannot infer from social media.
  • 4. Halt all financial movement: Never expedite wire transfers, OTP transmissions, or gift card purchases under urgent voice pressure.
