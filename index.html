About VoiceShield AI

Real-Time Detection and Prevention of Voice Cloning Impersonation Attacks

How VoiceShield AI Works

Modern voice cloning architectures synthesize speech by generating acoustic spectral frames through neural vocoders. VoiceShield AI analyzes physical vocal tract bio-signals, prosodic inflection variability, neuromuscular micro-jitter, and pause room-tone physics to differentiate authentic human speech from synthetic artifacts.

Acoustic Liveness & Replay Analysis

When an attacker plays pre-recorded audio through a secondary speaker into a microphone, acoustic comb filtering, double reverberation, and frequency attenuation occur. VoiceShield AI inspects these transfer functions to rate acoustic replay risk.

Ethical Standards & System Limitations

AI voice detection is probabilistic. Quality degradations (such as extreme cellular compression or heavy stadium noise) reduce acoustic boundary certainty. VoiceShield AI acts as an enterprise decision-support tool and never claims 100% detection accuracy.
