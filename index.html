Caller Voice Trap Challenge

AI voice cloners and text-to-speech pipelines suffer latency delays and cannot easily respond to dynamic unprompted cognitive challenges.

Ask the caller right now:

“Can you spell the name of your first school backward, while tapping your desk three times?”

What to look for:
  • Unusual pause or robotic latency glitch (>2.5 seconds)
  • Inability to process non-standard phonetic tasks
  • Abrupt disconnect or repetition of generic script
